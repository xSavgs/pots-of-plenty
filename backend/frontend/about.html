WHO WE ARE

Fresh seafood.
Real people.
Real work.

We’re a family-run fishing business in Scarborough, hauling lobsters and crabs for decades from the waters of the North Sea.

Every catch is landed fresh and available for collection directly from Scarborough Harbour. No middlemen, no warehouses — just fresh local seafood straight from the boat.

Alongside the seafood, Pots of Plenty represents the lifestyle around the harbour — early mornings, rough seas, hard graft and the community built around it.
